Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an OpenAI or other AI key? ▾

No. NEXUS uses Puter.js, which provides AI access to every signed-in user — no API keys, no billing setup.

Where are my projects saved? ▾

Signed in, projects sync to your private Puter cloud. As a guest, they live in your browser's local storage.

Can I export my code? ▾

Yes. Every project exports as a clean ZIP (HTML / CSS / JS) you can host anywhere.

Does it work on Android phones? ▾

It's designed mobile-first. We tested on entry-level Android devices — touch gestures, voice prompts, and a floating bottom toolbar are all built-in.

Can I publish to a custom domain? ▾

Yes — every site gets a free Puter subdomain. Custom domain support is on the Pro plan.
